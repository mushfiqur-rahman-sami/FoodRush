Privacy policy

What stays on your phone

Your cart, favourites, recent searches, profile photo, delivery address and a copy of your orders.

Accounts and orders

When the app is connected to Firebase, your name, email, phone number, role, orders, reviews and chats are stored in that Firebase project. Otherwise they stay on this phone, with your password stored only as a salted hash.

Phone numbers

Your phone number is shared only with the people on your order: the customer, the rider and the restaurant.

Location

Customers' locations are used to set the delivery address. Riders' locations are shared, while they are online, with the customer of their current delivery so the customer can follow the food.

Calls

In-app calls go directly between the two phones. FoodRush does not record them.

Payment details

Card numbers and wallet numbers you type into the demo forms are checked for format and then discarded. Only a label such as the last digits is kept.

Other services

Map tiles load from OpenStreetMap; addresses from Photon, Nominatim or Android's Geocoder; roads from the Overpass API; and backup routes from OSRM. Those services see your IP address and the places you look up.
